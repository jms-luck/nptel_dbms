Q13

Consider two relations r 1 and r 2 with schemas R 1 and R 2 , respectively. Here, | R 1 | = m and | R 2 | = n and m >= 2, n >= 2. Relation r 3 is the natural join of r 1 and r 2 ; i.e. r 3 = r 1 r 2 . Suppose r 1 and r 2 share k number of common attributes, where k > 0 and k < min(m,n). Identify the FALSE statement:

  • The number of attributes in r 3 will be less than m+n.
  • The minimum number of attributes in r 3 is: max(m,n).
  • The scheme of r 3 will be: R 1 ( R 2 { a 1 , a 2 , . .., a k }) , where a 1 to a k are common attributes.
  • The maximum possible number of attributes in r 3 is: m+n-1.

Answer: The minimum number of attributes in r 3 is: max(m,n).
