Q1

Prerequisite / Context: Use the following notation for this assignment.

Which of the following schedules follow the principles of Two Phase Locking?

  • L 1 (X), L 1 (Y), L 2 (Z), U 2 (Z), U 1 (Y), U 1 (X)
  • L 1 (X), U 1 (X), L 1 (Y), L 2 (Z), U 2 (Z), U 1 (Y)
  • L 1 (X), U 1 (X), L 1 (Y), U 1 (Y), L 2 (Z), U 2 (Z)
  • L 1 (X), L 1 (Y), L 2 (Z), U 2 (Z), U 1 (Y), U 1 (X), L 2 (X), U 2 (X)

Answer: L 1 (X), L 1 (Y), L 2 (Z), U 2 (Z), U 1 (Y), U 1 (X)
