Notations

L_i(X)   Lock on X by transaction i
U_i(X)   Unlock X
Req_i(X)  Request lock on X
R_i(X)   Read X
W_i(X)   Write X
C_i      Commit

Questions

1. Schedule satisfying Two Phase Locking:

a) L1(X), L1(Y), L2(Z), U2(Z), U1(Y), U1(X)
b) L1(X), U1(X), L1(Y), L2(Z), U2(Z), U1(Y)
c) L1(X), U1(X), L1(Y), U1(Y), L2(Z), U2(Z)
d) L1(X), L1(Y), L2(Z), U2(Z), U1(Y), U1(X)

Answer: L1(X), L1(Y), L2(Z), U2(Z), U1(Y), U1(X)

2. Deadlock schedule:

a) Req1(X), L1(X), Req2(X), L2(Y), Req1(Y)
b) Req1(X), L1(X), Req2(X), Req1(Y), L2(Y), Req1(Y)
c) Req1(X), L1(X), Req2(Y), L2(Y), Req1(Y), Req2(X)
d) Req1(X), L1(X), Req2(Y), L2(Y), Req1(Y), Req2(X)

Answer: Deadlock occurs when circular wait exists

3. UNDO logging value of A after restart:

a) 10
b) 30
c) 20
d) Can't determine

Answer: 30

4. UNDO logging value of B after recovery:

a) 20
b) 40
c) 10
d) Can't determine

Answer: Can't be determined

5. REDO logging value of B:

a) 20
b) 40
c) Must be 40
d) Must be 20

Answer: Must be 40

6. Logging method ignoring incomplete transactions:

a) UNDO
b) REDO
c) UNDO-REDO
d) All

Answer: REDO

7. UNDO-REDO order:

a) Undo then Redo
b) Redo then Undo
c) Parallel
d) None

Answer: Undo: S then R, Redo: T then U

8. FALSE statement:

a) Strict Cascadeless
b) Cascadeless Recoverable
c) Strict Serializable
d) Recoverable Serializable

Answer: Recoverable Serializable (False)

9. Schedule type:

a) Strict
b) Cascadeless
c) Cascadeless not strict
d) None

Answer: Cascadeless and not strict

10. Number of valid commit orders:

a) 0
b) 3
c) 6
d) 8

Answer: 6
