Q5

  • Single valued
  • Multi valued
  • Composite
  • Single-valued and derived

Answer: Single-valued and derived

Q6

Suppose WorksFor is a relationship type with two participating entity types Employee and Company . Say, it represents the full-time job of the employees. What is the appropriate cardinality ratio for Employee: Company?

  • 1:1
  • N:1
  • 1:N
  • M:N

Answer: N:1

Q7

Suppose BankAccount is a relationship type with two participating entity types Person and Account in a bank. What is/are the possible cardinality ratio(s) for Person: Account? P: 1-to-1; Q: 1-to-N; R: N-to-1; S: M-to-N

  • Only P
  • Only P & Q
  • Only P, Q & R
  • All cardinality ratios are possible.

Answer: All cardinality ratios are possible.

Q8

Suppose Assigned is a many-to-one relationship type with two participating entity types Employee and Department . A maximum of seven employees are assigned to a department. If in the ER diagram, the line that connects Employee to the relationship type Assigned is to be labeled with (x,y) using min-max notation, choose the correct option:

  • x = 1, y = 7
  • x = 1, y = 1
  • x = 7, y = 1
  • x = 7, y = 7

Answer: x = 1, y = 1

Q10

Q12

Consider two entity sets A = {a,b,c,d} and B = {w,x,y,z} and they participate in a relationship R. The instances of R are given as: {(a,w),(a,x),(c,y),(d,z),(c,z)}. Which one of the following is correct:

  • Cardinality ratio of A:B is one-to-many; A participates partially; B participates completely
  • Cardinality ratio of A:B is many-to-one; A participates completely; B participates completely
  • Cardinality ratio of A:B is one-to-many; A participates completely; B participates partially
  • Cardinality ratio of A:B is many-to-many; A participates partially; B participates partially

Answer: Cardinality ratio of A:B is one-to-many; A participates partially; B participates completely

Q13

Consider two entity sets A = {a,b,c,d,e} and B = {w,x,y,z} and consider a relation R as a many-to-one relationship from entity set A to entity set B. Assume that A and B participate totally in R. Which one of the following is TRUE about R:

  • Every entity in A is associated with exactly one entity in B
  • Some entity in A is associated with more than one entity in B
  • Every entity in B is associated with exactly one entity in A
  • Every entity in B is associated with at most one entity in A

Answer: Every entity in A is associated with exactly one entity in B
