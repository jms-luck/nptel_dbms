1. An entity type may have more than one attribute.

a) True
b) False

Answer: True

2. A relationship type may have more than one attribute.

a) True
b) False

Answer: True

3. A strong entity type always has a key.

a) True
b) False

Answer: True

4. A weak entity type always has a key.

a) True
b) False

Answer: False

5. Total-Salary (Base + HRA + Allowances) is:

a) Single valued
b) Multi valued
c) Composite
d) Single-valued and derived

Answer: Single-valued and derived

6. Cardinality for WorksFor (Employee Company):

a) 1:1
b) N:1
c) 1:N
d) M:N

Answer: N:1

7. Possible cardinalities for BankAccount (Person, Account):

a) Only P
b) Only P & Q
c) Only P, Q & R
d) All cardinalities

Answer: All cardinalities are possible

8. Assigned (Employee Department) max-min notation:

a) (1,7)
b) (1,1)
c) (7,1
d) (7,7)

Answer: (1,1)

9. Statements:

S1: Owner of weak entity is strong entity
S2: Entity sets must be disjoint

a) Both True
b) S1 True
c) S2 True
d) Both False

Answer: Both False

10. Matching sets (ER concepts):

a) p-w, q-r, r-s, s-z
b) p-y, q-w, r-x, s-z
c) p-y, q-r, r-x, s-z
d) p-y, q-w, r-x, s-x

Answer: p-y, q-w, r-x, s-z

11. FALSE statement:

a) A1 may be key of customer
b) A2 may be key of customer
c) A3 may be key of airport
d) A4 may be key of student

Answer: A2 may be key of customer

12. Relationship R instances:

a) 1:1
b) 1:N
c) N:1
d) M:N

Answer: N:1 (A partial, B total)

13. Many-to-one relationship A B implies:

a) Each A exactly one B
b) Some A multiple B
c) Each B one A
d) Each B many A

Answer: Every entity in A is associated with exactly one entity in B
